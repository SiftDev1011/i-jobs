# About i-jobs

i-jobs by 99 finds remote jobs for people across Africa and applies for them, within rules each person sets.

## Why we built it

Good remote jobs exist, but finding them takes hours, and most applications are sent into silence. Job communities on WhatsApp and Telegram already share hundreds of jobs a week. i-jobs turns that sharing into real applications: every member gets a profile from their CV, a daily list of jobs that fit, and an autopilot that applies while they get on with their day.

## How access works

i-jobs comes through communities. A community owner runs a job group, sets a membership price, and earns from every member who pays. Every community is checked by a person before it goes live, and so is every company that posts jobs directly.

## What we won’t do

- We never charge anyone to apply for a job, and we remove jobs that do.
- We never show community owners their members’ phone numbers, emails or CVs.
- We never apply to a job a member has ruled out.
